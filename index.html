Safari Details & Pricing

Hi,

We organize full day, half day, and night safari game rides. Our rates are as follows:

  • Full Day Safari (12 hrs): Rs 42,000/= (Includes packed lunch for 2 pax only)
  • Half Day Safari (4 ½ to 5 hrs): Rs 21,000/= (For 2 pax only)
  • Night Safari (4 hrs): Rs 20,000/= (Valid for up to 6 pax)

All our drivers are very well trained, reliable, and highly experienced in order to guarantee our guests the best wildlife experience.

* Above prices for Full Day and Half Day safaris are for 2 pax only. We will arrange a price and let you know if there are more guests (for full day safari). Night Safari rate covers up to 6 pax.

Important Information:

  • Kindly settle the above amounts only to the hotel.
  • We will not be responsible if the above mentioned amounts are paid to anyone else other than the hotel.
  • You will also require Sri Lankan Rupees to purchase your entrance fee tickets from the National Park office for your safari.
  • Also kindly be informed that above rates are EXCLUDING the entrance fee.
  • No hidden charges.

Authentic Village Walk Experience

Village Walk Photo Gallery

Experience Real Rural Life & Nature

Immerse yourself in the serene beauty and rich cultural heritage of rural Sri Lanka with our guided Traditional Village Walk. Designed to offer a peaceful escape into nature, this walk allows you to explore the untouched surroundings around Kurumba Lodge.

Price: Rs 5,000/= | Duration: 2 Hours

Tour Highlights:

  • Scenic Lake Trails: Stroll along lush pathways bordering quiet local lakes (Wewa), enjoying fresh breezes and picturesque landscapes.
  • Lush Jungle Borders: Walk alongside natural forest patches, observing wild flora, tropical plants, and native wildlife.
  • Bird Watching Opportunities: Spot vibrant local and migratory bird species inhabiting the lake areas and tree canopies.
  • Ancient Temple Visits: Visit serene rural Buddhist temples, experience peaceful spiritual surroundings, and learn about local traditions.
  • Authentic Rural Life: Interact with friendly villagers and experience the authentic, everyday lifestyle of Sri Lankan village culture.

Whether you want a refreshing morning stroll or a peaceful late afternoon sunset walk, our Village Walk offers a memorable connection with Sri Lanka's nature and heritage.

Important Notes:

  • Comfortable walking shoes and lightweight cotton clothing are recommended.
  • Sun protection (hat/sunglasses) and insect repellent are advised.
  • When visiting local temples, please wear clothing that covers shoulders and knees.
  • Kindly settle payment directly with the hotel management.
